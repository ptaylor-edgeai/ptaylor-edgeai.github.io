I am a PhD candidate at [Capitol Technology University](https://www.captechu.edu/) (Maryland, expected 2027),
researching **Decentralised Federated Learning (DFL)** and **Edge AI** — specifically how
resource-constrained devices can collaborate on model training and unlearning without a central
coordinator. My work sits at the intersection of distributed systems, privacy-preserving machine
learning, and ultra-constrained hardware.

My current research focuses on **RAD-FL** (Resource-Aware Decentralised Federated Learning),
a framework implemented in Swift and deployed on Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W clusters. I am also investigating
empirical resource characterisation of federated machine unlearning on sub-watt edge hardware.

This research is grounded in four decades of industry experience across IT consulting and
enterprise systems architecture, with clients spanning financial services, automotive, and
professional services sectors in Australia and internationally. That production-systems depth
shapes how I approach research problems: with an emphasis on practical deployability, resource
constraints, and real-world trade-offs that pure theory often overlooks.

**Research interests:** Decentralised federated learning &middot; Machine unlearning &middot;
Edge AI &middot; Privacy-preserving ML &middot; Resource-constrained systems &middot;
Non-IID data in distributed settings

I hold a Master of Data Science from [James Cook University](https://www.jcu.edu.au/).
